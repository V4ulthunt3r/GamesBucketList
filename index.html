How it works

From “I should play that” to actually playing it.

Three steps, and none of them is “make an account”.

1

Capture it anywhere

Share a link or a bit of text from Safari, a video app or a group chat straight into your library — the share sheet does the rest. Prefer to type? Search the built-in game catalog by title.

2

Sort it your way

Planned, Playing, Completed, Paused or Dropped. Add a rating out of five, a note for later, and group games into lists for co-op nights, a holiday or your all-time favourites.

3

See the whole picture

Insights turns the library into something you can read at a glance: how your backlog is balanced, which genres and platforms you actually pick, and how you rate what you finish.

Features

Built for a backlog that keeps growing.

Everything below is in version 1.0 — no add-ons, no upsell in the middle of a sentence.

A game catalog that fills in the blanks

Type a title and the catalog adds platform, genre, release year and cover art. When a game exists in several editions you pick the right match yourself.

Save from any app

The share extension turns a link or a snippet of text into a library entry in one tap. The source stays attached, so you can always jump back to where you found it.

Five honest statuses

Planned, Playing, Completed, Paused, Dropped. Because “I bounced off it after two hours” deserves a place in the library too.

Ratings and notes

Tap a star to rate it out of five and write down what stuck — the ending, the soundtrack, the friend who talked you into it.

Lists for every occasion

Group games for co-op nights, trips, favourites or whatever comes next — each list with its own colour and icon. A game can belong to more than one list.

Insights, not homework

A ring or bar chart of your library balance, your top genres and platforms, and your average rating. Generated from what you already saved — nothing extra to fill in.

Can't decide? Roll for it

Pick Random Game takes one entry out of your list and hands it to you. Evening sorted, no scrolling required.

Your iCloud, your library

The library lives on your device and syncs to your other devices through your own iCloud account. No iCloud, or no signal? Everything keeps working locally.

Make it yours

Tiles or carousel, light or dark, English or German. Add your name and an avatar — a photo, or one you create with Image Playground on supported devices.

A look inside

Familiar where it counts, quiet everywhere else.

Native SwiftUI, light and dark, and a slow wash of colour drawn from whichever cover is on screen. Your covers set the mood; the app stays out of the way.

Catalog — one tap to save

No account. No tracking. No ads.

A bucket list is a private thing. Games Bucket List has no login, ships without an analytics SDK, and never sells the fact that you still haven't finished that RPG.

Stays on your device

Your games, ratings, notes and lists are stored locally. There is no server of mine holding your library.

Syncs through your iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, your library moves between your devices in your own private CloudKit database — the same place your other Apple data lives.

Only the lookup travels

Looking up a title sends the catalog service that title — for a shared link, the platform it came from and the game's ID there. Never your library, your ratings or your notes.
